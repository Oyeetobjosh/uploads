# Episode 01 — "Why the Tortoise Has a Cracked Shell"

## ACT 1 — THE HOOK (0:00 – 0:50)
**Visual:** Slow zoom into cracked tortoise shell → snap to title card (savanna, baobab tree)

- **VO:** Have you ever looked at a tortoise and wondered… why is his shell cracked? Why is it patched together like an old pot mended a thousand times? Sit close, little ones, and I will tell you the story of Ajapa the Tortoise — the cleverest… and the greediest animal in all the land.
- **SFX:** soft drum intro, whoosh on title

## ACT 2 — THE DREAM (0:50 – 3:00)
**Scene A:** Village by the river, morning. Ajapa crawling through grass, smooth shiny shell.
**Scene B:** Birds swooping in blue sky. Ajapa's longing face.
**Scene C:** Night — Ajapa looking at moon, dreaming of flying.

- **VO:** Long ago, in a green village beside the great river, Ajapa's shell was smooth and shiny, like a polished stone. But Ajapa was never happy with what he had. He wanted more. Much more. One morning, as he crawled through the tall grass, he heard the birds above him. Whoosh! Whoosh! They swooped and soared across the bright blue sky. "Oh," sighed the tortoise, "if only I could fly! Up there, the whole world is waiting. Down here, it is only dust and mud." That night he could not sleep. He stared at the moon and dreamed of wings.
- **SFX:** birdsong, wind, night crickets

## ACT 3 — THE DEAL (3:00 – 5:00)
**Scene D:** Ajapa bowing to the King of Birds (great hornbill, orange beak).
**Scene E:** Every bird donating a feather — red, blue, yellow.
**Scene F:** Village children laughing as Ajapa ties feathers on with vines. Close-up of determined face. He launches — up, up, up!

- **VO:** So Ajapa crawled to the king of the birds, a great hornbill with a mighty orange beak. "Your Majesty," he said, bowing low, "lend me just one feather from each of your people. With them I will build wings, and when you fly to the great feast in the sky, I will fly beside you." The birds' hearts were kind, and the tortoise looked so small. "Very well," said the king. And one by one, every bird gave Ajapa a feather. Red feathers, blue feathers, yellow feathers! Ajapa tied them to his legs with strong vine, and when he finished, he looked just like a bird himself. The village children laughed. "A tortoise cannot fly!" they giggled. But Ajapa stretched his neck, flapped his feathered legs… and up, up, up he rose — over the trees, over the hills, into the clouds! The tortoise was flying!
- **SFX:** drum roll on launch, whoosh

## ACT 4 — THE FEAST IN THE SKY (5:00 – 7:15)
**Scene G:** Flying above clouds with the flock, golden sun.
**Scene H:** The feast — tables as long as rivers, yams, honey, fruits, roasted corn.
**Scene I:** The feast-name moment. Ajapa's sneaky smile. "My name is All-of-You."
**Scene J:** Food arrives — Ajapa grabs everything. Birds shocked, empty plates.

- **VO:** The birds flew with him higher and higher, until they reached the great feast in the sky. And what a feast it was! Tables as long as rivers, piled high with golden yams and sweet honey, ripe fruits and roasted corn. The smell made Ajapa's nose twitch with joy. But before the eating began, the host bird announced, "Here in the sky, every guest must carry a feast name. Tell us, tortoise, what is your name?" Ajapa smiled his sneakiest smile. "My name," he said slowly, "is All-of-You." The birds laughed. What a funny name! Then the food arrived, carried by silver clouds. The host raised his wings and called, "This delicious food is for all of you!" And before anyone could move, Ajapa jumped up and shouted, "All of you? That is ME!" He grabbed the biggest bowl and gobbled everything. The honey! The yams! The fruits! Not one crumb was left. The birds sat in silence, their plates empty, their hearts cold.
- **SFX:** party music, gobbling, gasps

## ACT 5 — THE FALL (7:15 – 8:30)
**Scene K:** Angry birds taking back their feathers, one by one.
**Scene L:** Ajapa at the edge of the cloud, earth far below. He jumps.
**Scene M:** Tumbling through clouds. CRASH onto the ground.

- **VO:** Then the birds grew angry. Very, very angry. "You tricked us, tortoise!" they cried. And one by one, each bird took back his feather. Red feathers, blue feathers, yellow feathers. Soon Ajapa had no wings left. He stood at the edge of the sky, looking down at the earth far, far below. There was only one way home. Ajapa closed his eyes… and he jumped. Down, down, down he fell, tumbling through the clouds, faster and faster, until he crashed onto the hard earth with a great CRACK!
- **SFX:** wing flutters, whoosh, big crash + impact

## ACT 6 — THE MORAL & OUTRO (8:30 – 9:45)
**Scene N:** Cracked-shell tortoise on grass, birds gathered gently around. Sunset.
**Scene O:** End card — "The End" + subscribe logo.

- **VO:** The birds flew down and found him. He was alive — but his beautiful smooth shell was cracked into many pieces. And that is why, to this very day, every tortoise carries a cracked shell. It is a reminder that greed never pays… and that kindness is worth more than a thousand feasts. The end. If you enjoyed this story, subscribe for more African folktales — and tell us in the comments: what would you have named yourself at the sky feast?
- **SFX:** warm music swell, outro chime
