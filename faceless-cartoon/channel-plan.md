# Faceless Cartoon Channel — Honest Plan & Math

## The truth about "$50K/month YouTube automation" ads
- **~3% of faceless automation channels ever reach monetization** (1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours). 95%+ of all channels never qualify. [frameloop.ai 2026]
- The people selling $50K/month are mostly selling *courses*, not running channels. The $50K stories that are real took **years + paid teams + many failed channels**.
- Your $1,000–5,000 goal is **realistic** — but on this timeline:

| Stage | Monthly income | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Building (no income) | $0 | Months 1–7 |
| First monetization | $50–500 | Months 7–12 |
| Growing channel | $500–2,000 | Months 12–18 |
| Established + diversified | $2,000–5,000+ | Months 18–36 |

## The math you must understand (kids cartoons pay low!)
- Kids/"Made for Kids" content RPM ≈ **$0.50–$2 per 1,000 views** (behavioral ads banned by law).
- Family content marked "not made for kids" ≈ **$2–4.50 RPM**.
- **$1,000/month needs ~250,000–500,000 views/month** at those rates. **$5,000/month needs ~1.5–3.5M views/month.** That's hit-channel territory — achievable with a breakout video + library compounding, but plan for it.
- Hedge: same production skills, higher-paying niches later (finance/animation hybrids, storytelling $15–25 CPM).

## Weekly production system (2 videos/week → 8/month)
1. **Monday:** pick folktale (list of 50+ Nigerian/African tales ready), write script (like episode-01-script.md)
2. **Tuesday:** generate 10–14 scene images (AI image tool, consistent character style sheet)
3. **Wednesday:** voiceover recording (free: CapCut TTS; paid: ElevenLabs ≈ $5–22/mo — worth it)
4. **Thursday:** assemble in CapCut (free) — Ken Burns zoom, captions, music from YouTube Audio Library
5. **Friday:** thumbnail (Canva free) + SEO title/description + schedule upload
6. **Saturday:** cut 1–2 Shorts from the video → funnel to channel
7. **Sunday:** engage with comments, check analytics

## Golden rules
1. **Consistency beats perfection** — 2 videos/week for 6 months minimum. The #1 failure point is quitting in months 4–6.
2. **Never buy a "YouTube automation course."** Everything they sell is in this file + free tutorials.
3. **Make it original** — your own script + own voiceover + own images = safe from "reused content" demonetization (the #1 killer of AI channels).
4. **One niche, one style** — kids' attention = recognizable characters. Reuse the same tortoise design every episode.
5. **Diversify at month 12+** — affiliates (kids' books, audiobooks), licensing, channel flips.
